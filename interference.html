Constructive and destructive interference

Overlapping pressure waves reinforce when compressions meet compressions or rarefactions meet rarefactions. A compression overlapping a rarefaction opposes it. Equal waves a half cycle apart cancel at the listener.

Compare the traces on the same scale. The reference guides show the original amplitude. At a quarter-cycle offset, use the marker to see reinforcement at some instants and opposition at others.

Why it matters in a room

A reflection travels a different path to the listener, so it can arrive at a different phase. Moving can change the relation between direct and reflected waves. A weaker reflection in opposite phase reduces the direct sound without canceling it completely.
