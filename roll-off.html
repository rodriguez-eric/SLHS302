Roll-off for all four filter types

Roll-off describes how quickly gain falls along the attenuating slope. One octave doubles or halves the frequency. New gain = starting gain − (number of octaves × rate). Stay on the same slope. In the band-stop example, stop at the deepest point.

At −3 dB with a 6 dB/octave slope, −3 − 6 = −9 dB after one octave and −3 − 12 = −15 dB after two. A 6 dB drop roughly halves pressure and quarters intensity. A 3 dB drop roughly halves intensity.
