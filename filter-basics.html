What Does a Filter Do?

Read the filter response

Read the filter response

An air column responds strongly near resonance. For a mass on a spring, more mass lowers the natural frequency and more stiffness raises it. Filters shape the levels of frequency components.

A gain of 0 dB passes the component unchanged. Negative gain attenuates it and positive gain amplifies it. Add gain to the input level at the same frequency. A 1,000 Hz component at 60 dB SPL with gain −12 dB comes out at 48 dB SPL at 1,000 Hz.
