How do we combine levels?

For independent sources measured at the same listening position, convert each level to linear intensity using the lab model. Add IA + IB, then convert the total to a level.

IA = 10⁻¹² × 10^(LA/10), and likewise for IB. Then Ltotal = 10 log₁₀[(IA + IB)/10⁻¹²].

60 + 6010⁻¹² × 10⁶ = 10⁻⁶10⁻¹² × 10⁶ = 10⁻⁶2 × 10⁻⁶10 log₁₀(2 × 10⁶) ≈ 63.01 dB SPL
70 + 6010⁻¹² × 10⁷ = 10⁻⁵10⁻¹² × 10⁶ = 10⁻⁶1.1 × 10⁻⁵10 log₁₀(1.1 × 10⁷) ≈ 70.41 dB SPL
80 + 5010⁻¹² × 10⁸ = 10⁻⁴10⁻¹² × 10⁵ = 10⁻⁷1.001 × 10⁻⁴10 log₁₀(1.001 × 10⁸) ≈ 80.004 dB SPL

Enter values such as 1.1e-5 for intensity. Round combined levels to two decimals. For equal sources, +3 dB is also accepted.
